A PERSONAL ASSISTANT, RUN LOCALLY

Your tools.
Your own computer.

Kindi Headless is a self-hosted assistant for working with files, checking hardware and using connected Google services from a private installation.

Local by design

The language model runs on the operator’s computer. The remote interface is accessed through a private network.

Connected when needed

With permission, Kindi can read Gmail, prepare drafts and work with Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets, Slides, Tasks, Contacts and Forms.

You review email

Kindi prepares email drafts. Sending a message requires the operator to review it and confirm the send.

This is an information page. It does not provide access to a Kindi installation, collect Google credentials or connect visitors’ accounts.
